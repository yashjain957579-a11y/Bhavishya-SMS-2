Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information Bhavishya SMS ("we", "us") collects and how we use it, on this website (the Century in 75 page, exit quiz, leaderboard and practice sheets) and in Bhavishya uploader, our internal tool that uses YouTube API Services.

1. Bhavishya uploader and YouTube API Services

Bhavishya uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Who uses it: only the owner of the Bhavishya SMS YouTube channel, on their own computer, to upload and schedule the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • What it accesses: with the owner's permission (Google sign-in, OAuth 2.0) it uploads videos with their title, description, tags and schedule (scope youtube.upload), and reads the channel's name and its own recent uploads to confirm their status (scope youtube.readonly).
  • What it stores: a sign-in token on the owner's computer, and a local log of the videos it uploaded (file name, video ID, title, publish time). Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • What it does not do: it does not read, collect, store or share any data about viewers or any other YouTube user, does not show YouTube content to anyone, and does not use data for advertising.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time from Google Account security settings (third-party access). Deleting the local token file also ends access.
  • Deletion: stored API data (the token and the upload log) is deleted on request, or within 30 days after access is revoked.

2. This website and the exit quiz

  • When you take an exit quiz we collect the name and WhatsApp number you enter and your quiz answers and scores. They are stored in our private Google Sheet.
  • Your name may appear on the public leaderboard. Your WhatsApp number is never shown; we use it to keep your scores together and to send Century in 75 updates.
  • If you buy a pass, payment is handled by Razorpay under its own privacy policy; we receive your name, contact number and payment status, not your card or bank details.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with third parties, except the service providers named above.
